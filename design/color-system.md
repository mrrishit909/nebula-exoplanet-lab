# Color system: instrument and starlight

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Void | #06050A | Page, canvas clear colour, the intro's opening |
| Ultraviolet | #7B4DFF | Orbit lines, water bands, system label, rules |
| Solar Orange | #FF7A1A | Readouts, planet A in spectra, hot worlds, section labels |
| Spectral Pink | #EE3D8A | Active station, sliders, transit cursor, discovery bars |
| Starlight | #FFF4D9 | Text, the light curve, orbit of the selected planet |
| Instrument Gray | #25242C | Inactive timeline bars, panel lines |

The molecules take their colours from the same palette (water ultraviolet, carbon dioxide orange, methane pink, ozone starlight), so a highlighted band in the spectrum and its chip are the same colour. Decorative rules in the panels are spectra: tick marks at the molecule bands' proportions.
