# Motion spec

- Selecting a system is a camera journey: the camera arcs from where it is to the system's overview, 2.4 s, with a lift at mid-journey. No page change.
- Orbits draw progressively as the planet is observed (a per-planet accumulated fraction drives the line's draw range); the selected planet's orbit is brighter.
- The transit curve, the observer's view of the star and the 3D system are one clock: the cursor on the curve, the planet in front of the star disk and the star's brightness in the scene are computed from the same orbital angle with the same functions.
- The panel dividers are spectra. Panels open like a slit.
- Time filters reshape the sky: the year smooths toward its target (8 percent a frame), so stars fade in and out instead of popping.
- Ambient motion (planet spin, cloud drift, lava pulse, star granulation) stops under Pause Motion and reduced motion.
