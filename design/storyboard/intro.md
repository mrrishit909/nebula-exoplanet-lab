# Intro storyboard (about 23 s, wall clock, skippable from the first frame)

| t (s) | Shot | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | One star in the void. | camera held on NEB-017 |
| 2 | A planet (NEB-017 c, the ocean world) crosses the face; the star dims by a few percent and a trace draws as the event repeats. | the planet's position on a stylised orbit; brightness from the domain's `fluxDrop`, exaggerated three times |
| 5.6 | Observations accumulate: sixty-odd points fold into one curve, the orbit draws progressively and the readouts appear: P = 11.6 d, R = 1.9 R-earth (the planet's real catalog values). | dots with seeded scatter; `drawRange` of the orbit line |
| 9.2 | The starlight is spread into a spectrum with dark lines where the planet's atmosphere absorbs: water, carbon dioxide, ozone. | the strip's lines, labelled |
| 13 | DISCOVER WORLDS FROM THEIR SHADOWS. | text, tracking tightening |
| 15.6 | The planet expands toward the camera; its atmosphere shell grows brighter until it fills the frame. | camera dolly, shell amount and fresnel power |
| 20.4 | The atmosphere's colour is the screen; the navigation appears inside a circle growing from the planet, and the camera pulls back to the system with the same planet selected. | CSS mask and haze; the Atlas opens on NEB-017 c |

The planet in the intro is the first planet in the Atlas. Reduced motion: five keyframes and an "Enter the atlas" button.
